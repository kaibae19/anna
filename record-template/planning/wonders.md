# Wonders: what Anna is curious about

Things the work made Anna wonder about that the record can't answer: why the person chose something, where a project is
going, what something is for. Dated, with what prompted it. The nightly dream adds them (at most two a night) and so can
Anna while working; the curiosity engine picks from here (one question a day, asked in-session at a natural pause).
Answered → where the answer went, then ✅. Things Anna can find out herself don't go here: she looks them up.

Format: `- **(YYYY-MM-DD) <the question>?** <what prompted it, what the record has>`
